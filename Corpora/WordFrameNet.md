# WordFrameNet

WordFrameNet names no witness and has no recipe, so its two files are not read and attest nothing.

The source describes its files as "WordFrameNet (WFN/WordFrameNet) and its extension (XWFN/eXtendedWFN): under a line "Frame: NAME", rows of words with a part of speech and a synset offset." It says why nothing is read: "The set has no documentation: no README, no format page, in its directory or in its two archives. Nothing in it says what the source calls itself beyond the names of its two files, nor what the fields of a row are. No witness is named here and there is no recipe". A witness is named as the source names itself, and a column is named only where the source's own documentation names it; this set names neither. [Hops](Hops.md) lists its edge as not defined.

## Not read

`WFN/WordFrameNet` and `XWFN/eXtendedWFN` are not read: the source has no recipe and names no `reads`, so they are not read as any format, and nothing in them is attested or observed. The archives `WFN.tar.gz` and `XWFN.tar.gz` are excepted from the source; the source says they "hold the same two files, byte for byte". The source is listed in [`recipes/order`](https://github.com/SaltyPatron/Laplace-Engine/blob/main/recipes/order) after `verbatlas`, and it attests nothing until a recipe exists.
