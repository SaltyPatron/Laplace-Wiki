# Natural Earth

Natural Earth is a public-domain cartographic vector corpus queued for declarative `.recipe` admission (`Batch 2`, `Laplace#372`), attesting country, administrative, physical, and populated-place geometries alongside multilingual place names, ISO 3166 codes, and shared `WIKIDATAID` and `GEONAMESID` highway crosswalks.

## Source & `Batch 2` admission queue

Natural Earth ([naturalearthdata.com](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/)) provides 1:10m, 1:50m, and 1:110m cultural and physical vector themes (Shapefile `.shp`/`.dbf`/`.prj` or GeoPackage). Its declarative `.recipe` and vector geometry reader are queued in `Batch 2` (`Laplace#372`, alongside Wikidata and Wikipedia) under one `Natural Earth` witness trunk (`class UserCuratedResource` / `StandardsDerived`).

Under the Recording Law ([Corpora](README.md#page-shape), [30. Conflicts](../Sequence/Conflicts.md) S1):

- **Shared highway crosswalks (`WIKIDATAID`, ISO codes, `GEONAMESID`):** `WIKIDATAID` (`Q...`) and ISO 3166 codes (`ISO_A2`, `ISO_A3`, `ADM0_A3`) are shared content highway IDs that collide directly with [Wiktionary](Wiktionary.md), Wikidata, Wikipedia, [ISO 639](ISO-639.md) (`CLDR` territories), and [GeoNames](GeoNames.md) (`countryInfo.txt`), while `GEONAMESID` resolves onto GeoNames' `[name, latitude, longitude]` place nodes (`NE_ID` is Natural Earth's internal feature ID, resolved as `key` and recorded nowhere).
- **Geometry + multilingual lexicalizations vs. cartographic styling:** Polygon/line/point coordinates, `LATITUDE`/`LONGITUDE`, sovereign/admin hierarchy (`SOVEREIGNT`, `ADMIN`, `CONTINENT`, `SUBREGION`), population/GDP observations (`POP_EST`, `POP_MAX`, `GDP_MD` qualified by year), and the 25+ localized `NAME_<LANG>` columns (`NAME_EN`, `NAME_AR`, `NAME_ZH`, ...) supply canonical spatial trajectories and multilingual place lexicalizations, whereas map-coloring indices (`MAPCOLOR7..13`) and zoom/label-placement hints (`scalerank`, `LABELRANK`, `MIN_ZOOM`, `MIN_LABEL`, `MAX_LABEL`, `LABEL_X`, `LABEL_Y`) are cartographic rendering bookkeeping (`omit`).
