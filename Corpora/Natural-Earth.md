# Natural Earth

Most lines and areas contain embedded feature names, which are ranked by relative importance, with up to eight rankings per data theme.

## Value

| Attestation | What it means | Lands on | Value | Witness | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Admin 0 countries | Countries distinguish between metropolitan (homeland) and independent and semi-independent portions of sovereign states. Each country is coded with a world region that roughly follows the United Nations setup. Natural Earth shows de facto boundaries by default according to who controls the territory, versus de jure. The features page says countries are matched boundary lines and polygons with names attributes. | a country polygon in ne_110m_admin_0_countries | 177 DBF records. The publisher's page says there are 258 countries. NAME is one of the name fields in the DBF. | Natural Earth | [Admin 0 – Countries](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-countries/); [Features](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/features/); DBF header of ne_110m_admin_0_countries.dbf inside the zip |
| Populated places | Point symbols with name attributes. Includes all admin-0 capitals and some other major cities. The publisher says to use the PopMax column to size town labels, and says the 110m theme is derived from 10m populated places with SCALERANK <= 1 or FEATURECLA equal to Admin-0 capital or Admin-0 capital alt. | a populated-place point in ne_110m_populated_places | 243 DBF records. NAME and POP_MAX are columns in the DBF. | Natural Earth | [Populated Places](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-populated-places/); [Features](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/features/); DBF header of ne_110m_populated_places.dbf inside the zip |
