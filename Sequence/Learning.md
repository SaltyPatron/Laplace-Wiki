# 23. Learning

Learning is ingestion and consequence: every new observation is admitted, every observed consequence of an act is witnessed and folds into standing, firmware is rated by what it chose, and typed incompleteness proposes candidate extensions that activate only on evidence they did not manufacture; there is no gradient and no retraining.

Ingestion is the learning process. A training-corpus pass is admit, decompose, witness; learned parameters are explicit reusable identities plus witnessed standing; a checkpoint is the live substrate plus its rebuildable accelerators; fine-tuning is additional scoped evidence; a knowledge cutoff is the last admitted observation per source; catastrophic forgetting does not happen because old evidence remains addressable; alignment is explicit source, program, and resource law. Stochastic gradient descent is not reinvented, it is removed. Reinforcement learning is reinvented: an action's observed consequence is witnessed and folds into Glicko-2 standing, so what is reinforced is attributable standing, not an opaque weight change.

## Operations

### 23.1 Run OODA over the forward program

- **In:** a pass and what followed it.
- **Do:** OBSERVE is RESOLVE: the exact observation, discourse, open obligations, prior consequences. ORIENT is COUPLE then ORIENT: the typed response field and the joint interpretation, under the firmware's goals. DECIDE is ROUTE, SCAN, COMPOSE, PROPOSE, STEER, SELECT, under the firmware's policy. ACT is REALIZE and any effect under the authority and effect envelope. OBSERVE CONSEQUENCE is WITNESS of what happened, and the later outcomes that bear on it. OODA controls action selection and consequence closure; it does not create a new calculus, source truth, or a reusable instruction by itself.
- **Out:** the loop, `observe → couple/orient → decide/execute → realize → witness → update`, never retrieve-once-and-drain.
- **From:** `docs/specs/39_Personality_Firmware.md` §8.1; `docs/INVENTION.md` §13.

### 23.2 Keep the three rates apart

- **In:** any change.
- **Do:** fast cognition is one pass, query to calculate or search to semantic act, with no epoch or calculus change. Learning is observation to testimony to an adjudicated epoch: standing changes, calculus and firmware do not. Gödel discovery is a persistent typed fray to a candidate extension to disjoint evaluation to an activated version. None of the three can impersonate another in state or receipts.
- **Out:** each change labelled with its rate.
- **From:** `docs/specs/39_Personality_Firmware.md` §8.2.

### 23.3 Route feedback by lane

- **In:** a feedback signal.
- **Do:** the lane decides what may change. Fast cognition: a partial result, tool result, or open obligation inside the pass may change the pass's working frontier, never testimony, calculus, or firmware. Effect and outcome: an action's observed consequence may change occurrence and outcome state and firmware standing, never the claims' standing unless independent testimony bears on them. Evidence: independent later observations and testimony change claim standing as a new epoch, never calculus or firmware. Procedural: successful and failed pass trajectories become evidence for or against a program or firmware operation as reusable, never truth. Physical execution: latency, CPU, I/O, and crossings change eligibility for an accelerated path, never semantics. Gödel: persistent typed frays, failed predictions, and failed outcomes produce candidate extensions, and change nothing active until activation.
- **Out:** the change, in its lane.
- **From:** `docs/specs/39_Personality_Firmware.md` §7.

### 23.4 Rate the firmware by its consequences

- **In:** an observed consequence that bears on a pass.
- **Do:** firmware is an attributable participant; a pass under it is an occurrence attributed to it, as a game is attributed to its players. A user's correction, a confirmed answer, a tool or test result, an effect outcome, a later independently adjudicated contradiction of the answer, each is a matchup for the firmware that ran the pass, folding into its rating, deviation, volatility, and witnesses under [13. Consensus](Consensus.md). The outcome's witness is whoever observed the consequence, the user, the test runner, the tool; never the firmware and never Laplace grading itself. Consequences stay typed: a good act, a bad explanation, an overspent budget, and a later consequence are separate observations updating only the claims they bear on, never one success bit. Firmware standing never multiplies, gates, or re-rates the standing of the claims it read. Retrying a transport or replaying a read creates no matchup.
- **Out:** the firmware's standing.
- **From:** `docs/specs/39_Personality_Firmware.md` §6; `docs/plan/ASSIMILATION_ROADMAP.md` law 18.

### 23.5 Witness Laplace's own acts

- **In:** a response, a compile, a test, an executed action.
- **Do:** a generated response is itself content and may be witnessed with its derivation and receipt; it does not become authoritative truth because the system produced it. When Laplace codes and the compile fails, that failure is a witnessed negative outcome attached to the exact structure, toolchain, and environment; a passing compile is a positive one; each feeds the next iteration. Whether the response and the prompt mint attestations at all, or only observation, occurrence, and receipt state, and whether turn bookkeeping claims such as `APPEARS_IN`, `HAS_ATTRIBUTION`, and reply `DEPENDS_ON` prompt count as semantic attestations or record-lane structure, is conflict C1.
- **Out:** the act's outcome, witnessed by whoever observed it.
- **From:** `docs/INVENTION.md` §13; [Attestations: Outcomes](../Semantics/Attestations.md#outcomes); `docs/specs/39_Personality_Firmware.md` §9 C1.

### 23.6 Promote memory to skill to habit to muscle memory

- **In:** repeated proven passes.
- **Do:** memory is retained observations, passes, trajectories, outcomes, and receipts. A skill is a reusable, versioned cognition program proven under its activation contract. A habit is a firmware scheduling preference to propose a proven skill earlier under matching state; it changes proposal order and expected work, never truth, and contrary current state or preconditions defeat it. Muscle memory is a semantically equivalent accelerated path, a fused native operator, a perf-cache plane, a prepared provider, for a repeatedly proven procedure; it changes physical execution, never meaning, and keeps every authority and admissibility check.
- **Out:** the promotion level of the procedure.
- **From:** `docs/specs/39_Personality_Firmware.md` §8.4.

### 23.7 Detect a fray

- **In:** the shape of what is known.
- **Do:** the structure of what is known exposes what is missing, as Mendeleev's table predicted undiscovered elements: a periodic table of knowledge. A signal is a constrained vacancy, where repeated typed structure predicts that something with a particular signature belongs somewhere and no supported occupant is present; or a persistent contradiction, a failed prediction, a failed outcome. An empty query result is not a vacancy. "What new structure would explain why I couldn't answer correctly before?" Sense priors belong here, not in the engine attesting its own disambiguations.
- **Out:** a typed fray.
- **From:** `docs/specs/39_Personality_Firmware.md` §8.3 item 1; `docs/plan/ASSIMILATION_ROADMAP.md` law 19; `docs/INVENTOR_RECORD.md` §Gödel engine.

### 23.8 Propose a candidate

- **In:** the fray of 23.7.
- **Do:** propose a fact, relation family, motif, law, operator, cognition program, firmware operation, or calculus extension, carrying its predicted signature and complete ancestry. It is a derived hypothesis, not an observation.
- **Out:** the candidate.
- **From:** `docs/specs/39_Personality_Firmware.md` §8.3 item 2.

### 23.9 Evaluate disjointly

- **In:** the candidate.
- **Do:** fit it only on permitted evidence and evaluate it on pinned held-out evidence it did not manufacture; evaluation leakage fails the experiment. Search for counterexamples and charge its complexity. Self-generated descendants never corroborate the candidate that produced them.
- **Out:** a verdict, with its receipts.
- **From:** `docs/specs/39_Personality_Firmware.md` §8.3 item 3.

### 23.10 Activate by authority, fill by reality

- **In:** a candidate that survived 23.9.
- **Do:** it becomes active only by explicit activation authority, as a new version whose predecessor stays replayable. A predicted occupant does not fill its own vacancy: reality supplies an occurrence, testimony, or experiment outcome. A firmware version proposed this way is activated the same way and rated afterward by 23.4. Whether "Gödel engine" names this discovery lane alone or the whole self-referential loop is conflict C3.
- **Out:** the activated extension, or a rejected candidate kept as negative knowledge.
- **From:** `docs/specs/39_Personality_Firmware.md` §8.3 items 4 and 5, §9 C3.

## What this stage leaves behind

A world that grows by admission and by consequence, a firmware whose standing says how its choices turned out, and a lane by which the gaps in what is known become candidates that must earn their activation.

## Without this stage

Nothing Laplace does ever teaches it anything, every mistake is repeated, and every firmware is as good as any other.
