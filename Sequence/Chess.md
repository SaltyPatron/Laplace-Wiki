# 27. Chess

Chess is the proving domain: pieces, squares, moves, positions, lines, and games are content-addressed compositions above tier 0, a game is a trajectory, a move is a witnessed transition, analysis is versioned calculated testimony, play is the forward program, Stockfish is a pinned calculation provider and comparator, and cutechess is the neutral conductor of a falsifiable ladder.

Chess makes the common architecture falsifiable under exact rules and an unusually strong external reference. It gets no private identity, no private coordinate system, no private intelligence stack. The chess player ratings are not Glicko-2 standings of Laplace, and conflating the two is the defect that once put players above Carlsen.

The chain through [23. Learning](Learning.md). [7. Perfcaches](Perfcaches.md): the position and transition ROMs. [9. Sources](Sources.md): TWIC, Lichess openings, tablebases, PGN corpora as source generations.

## Operations

### 27.1 Compose the board vocabulary

- **In:** the rules.
- **Do:** Unicode remains tier 0. Pieces, squares, and their combinations are content-addressed entities at their compositional altitude, a finite piece-and-square vocabulary emitted as a dense ROM. A board state is a physicality trajectory over that vocabulary, never a FEN string masked as an entity; position identity includes the complete canonical state the modality requires, side to move, castling rights as one 8-bit mask whose centre bit is the king's file and whose sides are where it castles, en passant. History-dependent game state stays trajectory context, never salt.
- **Out:** the position recipe and the position ROM.
- **From:** `docs/specs/11_Chess_Provenance_Consensus_Spec.txt` §Identity ladder; `docs/guides/chess-forward-pass-proof.md` §Canonical chess content; `docs/INVENTOR_RECORD.md` §Chess.

### 27.2 Record games as trajectories

- **In:** PGN sources, books, openings.
- **Do:** a move is a transition from one position to another; a game is `P0 → M0 → P1 → M1 → …`, an ordered trajectory of reusable states and actions plus a distinct witnessed occurrence with players, event, clocks, result, and source. Transpositions converge on the same canonical positions while each game's occurrence stays attributable. Openings are starting sequences and tablebases are endings, both deduplicating and overlapping trajectories; an opening's Fréchet and a closing's Fréchet map to games. SAN and PGN are not recorded; they are generated by replay at the read edge. Zobrist keys are search accelerators, never identity.
- **Out:** positions, transitions, and game occurrences, and the transition ROM.
- **From:** `docs/guides/chess.md` §Identity and §Evidence lanes; `docs/INVENTIONS.md` #48, #98.

### 27.3 Separate the three lanes

- **In:** every chess fact.
- **Do:** the recorder lane records literal headers, participants, result, the typed move trajectory, annotations, clocks, and source metadata. The analyzer lane, replay-derived positions, transitions, motifs, evaluations, move quality, tablebase distance, self-play analysis, is a versioned calculated source. The runtime and self-play lane records played actions and outcomes under its own source and trust class. Player-name trust, game outcome, and opening membership are separate facts. A calculated move-quality judgment never overwrites or masquerades as the recorded result.
- **Out:** each fact in its lane.
- **From:** `docs/specs/11_Chess_Provenance_Consensus_Spec.txt` §Recorded testimony and §Calculated testimony.

### 27.4 Register Stockfish as a versioned calculation provider

- **In:** a Stockfish build.
- **Do:** a reproducible analysis is keyed by the canonical position id, the candidate move id when move-scoped, the Stockfish generation, binary digest, network identity, and calculation-affecting options, and the analysis recipe, fixed depth or nodes, MultiPV policy, tablebase boundary, adapter version. Results, centipawns, mate, WDL, candidate deltas, principal variations, depth, nodes, are calculated content. Ten thousand games that reach one position produce ten thousand observed contexts and one Stockfish opinion under one generation; a deterministic discrepancy on rerun is a provider health failure, never a reason to remint the position or count two results as corroboration. Stockfish is not a privileged epistemic class and not a hidden final move authority.
- **Out:** calculated planes: material, imbalance, phase, piece-square, pawn structure, king safety, mobility, motifs, tablebase WDL and DTZ, and Stockfish's own.
- **From:** `docs/guides/chess-forward-pass-proof.md` §Stockfish is a versioned calculation provider and §Dedup calculation, preserve occurrences.

### 27.5 Bridge across modalities

These are typed routes through one substrate, and no channel erases the others.

### 27.6 Play a move as a forward pass

- **In:** a board, a game, a request.
- **Do:** [20. Forward](Forward.md): RESOLVE the exact board, game, session, world boundary, and obligations; COUPLE every eligible chess and cross-modal plane without choosing a move, meaning, or provider first; ORIENT the task, play, explain, analyze, compare, find a line, prove a tactic; ROUTE providers, operators, and bounds, with Stockfish as one classical provider whose `bestmove` cannot secretly satisfy SELECT unless the operation is literally "return Stockfish bestmove"; SCAN, COMPOSE, PROPOSE legal candidates, STEER with witnessed evidence, line continuity, source scope, consensus, clocks, tablebase facts, and domain testimony, where standing may guide work allocation but cannot make an illegal move legal or override exact terminal state; SELECT; REALIZE the move into SAN, UCI, JSON, or the UI; WITNESS the action and its outcome. Each emitted move changes the active trajectory and the next coupling. The hot path is a bounded state fetch and one coarse native board and search operator, never a boundary per search node.

### 27.7 Fix the ruler with cutechess

- **In:** an experiment.
- **Do:** a frozen match generation binds the Stockfish opponent generation, the Stockfish analysis provider and its inclusion or holdout law, the cutechess version and orchestration, hardware, affinity, and serviceable-or-isolated resource profile, the paired colour-swapped opening suite, clock, depth, nodes, and adjudication law, the Laplace evidence epoch, and the Laplace firmware and operation recipe. cutechess owns process lifecycle, pairings, timing, adjudication, and PGN and result artifacts. Profiles stay distinct: deterministic single-thread analysis census; a pinned limited-strength calibration opponent, comparator evidence not a human Elo claim; one frozen fixed-reference opponent across a ladder; a host-max full-strength opponent with every setting pinned. State whether evaluated positions were covered by an admitted Stockfish generation or held out.
- **Out:** a replayable experiment.
- **From:** `docs/guides/chess-forward-pass-proof.md` §Stockfish experiment profiles and §Cute Chess experiment law.

### 27.8 Climb the ladder

- **In:** the ruler of 27.7.
- **Do:** add planes cumulatively and ablate: A0 legal and tactical proposal plus exact material; A1 plus piece-square and phase; A2 plus deterministic structure planes; A3 plus motifs, geometry, and tablebases; A4 plus optional Stockfish analysis; A5 plus derived structural residuals; A6 plus global PGN trajectory and outcome evidence; A7 plus player, opponent, rating, and time conditioning; A8 plus openings, books, and catalog providers; A9 plus lexical, sense, and cross-modal evidence; A10 the complete selected chess forward pass. For each rung compare `Ai` against `Ai−1` under matched resources, against the frozen reference, full against full-minus-one plane, and source ablations under identical challenges. Report W-D-L, Elo with uncertainty or SPRT, CPU, nodes, memory, wall, crashes and time losses, exact provider identities, and raw artifacts. Never promote a mid-run score to evidence.
- **Out:** measured evidence either way: a program that beats a pinned full-strength generation under a defensible protocol, or receipts that say which sources fail to add strength or cost too much.
- **From:** `docs/guides/chess-forward-pass-proof.md` §Cumulative/ablation proof ladder and §Scientific outcome.

### 27.9 Admit the results after the experiment closes

- **In:** the match artifacts.
- **Do:** new PGNs and outcomes are admitted into a later evidence epoch after the frozen comparison closes, through the runtime and self-play lane, and affect later decisions through ordinary testimony and fold. A benchmark never trains its own reference world mid-match unless that adaptive experiment is the explicit subject. Observed games do not self-certify as truth because Laplace produced them.
- **Out:** the next epoch.
- **From:** `docs/guides/chess-forward-pass-proof.md` §Cute Chess experiment law and §Acceptance.
