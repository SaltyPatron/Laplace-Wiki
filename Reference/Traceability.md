# Traceability

Every Sequence operation against the mechanism that performs it, the check that proves it, and its status.

The [Sequence](../Sequence/README.md) says what is done in what order; this page says, for each of its operations, what in the five repositories does it, what proves it, and whether it is **built** in the split repositories, **monorepo**, built in SaltyPatron/Laplace and not in the split repositories, **prototype** in Laplace-Prototype only, **specified** in the invention documents and not yet built in any repository the wiki documents, or **operator**, done by hand following the [Operations](../Operations/README.md) pages with no Laplace program doing it. A status with a qualifier says which part is which. Where the monorepo performs the operation, the mechanism says so after the words In the monorepo, whether or not the split repositories do too; the two implement the invention under different laws, listed in [Monorepo: How it differs](Monorepo.md#how-it-differs-from-the-split-repositories). Where the built machine differs from what the operation says, the mechanism column says how; the difference is recorded, not resolved.

Of the 283 operations: 137 are built in the split repositories at least in part, 125 are built in the monorepo and not in the split repositories, 3 exist in the prototype only, 5 are specified and built nowhere the wiki documents, and 13 are the operator's. A qualified status counts by its first word, so an operation built in part counts as built and a stage the monorepo builds in part counts as monorepo; the qualifier on each row says what is missing.

The same mechanism and status stand on each operation of the Sequence as its **Mechanism** line; this page is the index of them.
